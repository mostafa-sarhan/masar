# Phase E — Design proposal (step 1)

All three samples show the same real dashboard content (RTL Arabic, EGP, Egyptian names) so only the design differs.
Open the `.html` files in a browser, or see the `-desktop.png` / `-mobile.png` screenshots.

## Product names (Arabic + English)
| Name | Arabic | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Masar** | مسار | "Route / track": says tracking and routes; easy in both languages |
| **Mersal** | مِرسال | "Messenger, courier": a classic Arabic word for the one who delivers |
| **Wasla** | وصلة | "A leg / a connection" and close to "وصل" (arrived) |
| **Darb** | درب | "Path": short, strong, works as a logo word |
| **Kartona** | كرتونة | Egyptian for "carton box": friendly, local, memorable for merchants |

Trademark/domain checks are needed before picking.

## Direction A — Waybill (sample uses "Masar")
- **Mood:** calm, precise operations office; the day's work as a shipping manifest with rubber stamps.
- **Palette:** manifest paper `#EEF1F5`, ink navy `#1C2A4A`, carbon blue `#3550C8`, stamp red `#C8372D`, customs-tape yellow `#F2C230`.
- **Fonts:** Readex Pro for Arabic and Latin (one family, tabular numbers).
- **Signature:** "today's manifest" with a perforated edge; return/refusal reasons shown as tilted stamps.
- **Best for:** dispatchers and owners who live in the dashboard all day; reads serious and trustworthy.

## Direction B — Night Shift (sample uses "Mersal")
- **Mood:** a dispatch control room at night; couriers as lit roads across Cairo.
- **Palette:** night `#0E2730`, deck `#143640`, sodium lamp `#FFB547`, Nile teal `#3CC4B0`, coral `#FF7A6B`.
- **Fonts:** Alexandria for Arabic and Latin.
- **Signature:** each courier is a road with one light per stop (amber delivered, coral refused, dark pending).
- **Best for:** a strong demo and a distinctive brand; dark-first (light mode would be derived).

## Direction C — Kartona (sample uses "Kartona")
- **Mood:** friendly, local, merchant-facing; boxes and packing tape, Egyptian colloquial copy.
- **Palette:** white `#FFFFFF`, wash `#F3F5FA`, cobalt `#2346E8`, kraft `#C79B6D` / `#E9D3B8`, mint `#1FA97A`, cherry `#E2445C`.
- **Fonts:** Lalezar for Arabic display headings and numbers; Rubik for Arabic and Latin body text.
- **Signature:** count tiles as box fronts with packing tape; cash band as a taped kraft label.
- **Best for:** approachability for small merchants and couriers; the most "Egyptian" voice.

Whichever you pick, step 2 builds it with light and dark mode, RTL first and AA contrast.
